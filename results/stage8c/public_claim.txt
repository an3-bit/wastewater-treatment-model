In a virtual-plant study of a 30 m3/h textile wastewater RO system, WaterTwin's predictive decision framework was model-predicted to increase annual water recovery by 68.10% (recovering an additional 44,458 m3/year at 6.19% lower specific energy consumption) and create approximately KES 4,391,948/year of net economic value under the base tariff scenario. The result remains subject to pilot-scale industrial validation.
